# Photo Theme

Register: product

For Omarchy users who want to rediscover beautiful, meaningful personal photos on their desktop. The photo library can come from any provider through a local directory, or through an optional read-only Google Drive connection. Existing users must keep their photos, credentials, and preferences when migrating from the prototype.

The desktop should feel familiar and calm. Use Omarchy's existing background picker and Appearance menu. Keep personal photos local, make network syncing explicit, and never change another theme's wallpaper automatically. Pausing must remain paused across photo changes, theme changes, shell restarts, and updates. Avoid building a second wallpaper renderer or a photo gallery.
